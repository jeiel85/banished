📜 Lord's Guidebook (Ashborne)

1. Settling & Housing: Construct Dirt Roads to connect facilities. Build Burgage Plots so peasants have warm shelter. Upgrade their backyards into Vegetable Gardens or Chicken Coops to supply passive foodstuffs!

2. The 4 Seasons: In Spring, assign farmers to plow and sow wheat fields. In Summer, crops ripen. In Autumn, harvest grains swiftly. In Winter, the river freezes and farmland ceases—ensure you stockpile Firewood and food or your peasants will freeze and starve!

3. Production Chains: Raw Logs → Sawmill (Planks & Firewood). Wheat → Windmill (Flour) → Bakery (Bread). Iron Ore + Wood → Blacksmith (Spears & Bows). Diverse foods boost your Approval!

4. Militia & Fortress Defense: When raiders or rival knights approach, raise Spear Militia and Archer Militia from your drafted workers. Position Archers atop Watchtowers or behind Stone Walls for immense range and defensive multipliers.

5. Controls: Left-drag / WASD to pan camera. Mouse wheel to zoom. Left-click to select buildings or drag-box militia. Right-click to command militia movement and attack.
